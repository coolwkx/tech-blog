| LLaMA-7B | 7B | 32 | 4096 | 32 | 32000 | 1T | 2048 |
| BLOOM-176B | 176B | 70 | 14336 | 112 | 250880 | 366B | 2048 |

| Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct | 7B | 28 | 3584 | 28 query / 4 KV | 151936 | 18T（Qwen2.5 阶段） | 32768 |
